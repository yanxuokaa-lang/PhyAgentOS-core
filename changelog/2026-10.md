### 具体失败场景与现有机制不足 / Concrete Failure and Existing-Mechanism Gap
- `build_replan_delta()` 在模型提案前把失败节点后代闭包之外的 `red_grasp` 自动列入 `preserve_node_ids`；恢复提示同时要求 recovery Query 只提交当前恢复工作且不复制历史节点。模型据此返回单节点 replacement graph 后，Coordinator 正确拒绝了“preserve 元数据存在但 replacement 节点不存在”的 revision。
- `build_replan_delta()` automatically adds `red_grasp`, outside the failed node's descendant closure, to `preserve_node_ids` before model proposal. Recovery guidance simultaneously requires a recovery Query to contain only current recovery work and not copy historical nodes. When the model follows that guidance and returns a one-node replacement graph, Coordinator correctly rejects a revision whose preserve metadata names a missing replacement node.
- Git、版本号、主键、类型和普通 DAG 校验无法解决“候选 delta 在 replacement graph 产生前计算”的时序冲突；放宽 Coordinator 校验会重新允许虚假的 settlement carry-forward，而强制复制所有历史节点会违背分段恢复语义并扩大图。需要在纯 planner proposal 边界显式计算 effective delta。
- Git, versions, primary keys, types, and ordinary DAG validation cannot resolve the timing conflict created by computing a candidate delta before the replacement graph exists. Relaxing Coordinator admission would reallow false settlement carry-forward, while forcing all historical nodes into every recovery graph contradicts segmented recovery semantics and inflates the graph. An explicit effective-delta step is required at the pure planner proposal boundary.
